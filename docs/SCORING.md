# Direction Scoring
- Narrative clarity: Can a first-time reader understand what changed?
- Emotional impact: Does the sequence create the intended emotional arc?
- Visual composition: Are shot, angle, blocking, depth, and hierarchy purposeful?
- Pacing/scroll: Is time controlled through panel size and spacing?
- Reading flow: Are gaze, balloon order, and transitions natural?
- Continuity: Are characters, props, geography, lighting, and timeline coherent?

Scores are a quality gate and debugging instrument, not a replacement for creative judgment.
