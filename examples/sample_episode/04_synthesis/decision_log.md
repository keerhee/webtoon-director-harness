# Decision Log — sample_ep01

## North star
The reader must understand that the prototype is running **and** feel that Jun's presence is the
worse of the two facts. Comprehension of the room comes first; the emotional turn is at P05b.

## Spine
**Cinematic**, at 8.20 the highest-scoring candidate. It is the only candidate that establishes
geography, and geography is the precondition for every close-up the other two candidates rely on.

## Grafts

| Panel | Taken from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P03 | emotional | The silhouette-in-doorway was a genre default. Her face characterizes the dark; the doorway framing survives as composition, not as the beat. |
| P05a / P05b | emotional | The split is the scene's only dramatic irony — the reader reads the name before Mina does. Cinematic delivered both facts in one panel and spent the beat. |
| P05b crop | emotional | Cropping to the stopped hand makes the reader supply the face. Cinematic showed the face and turned the reader into an observer. |
| P04, P07 heights | webtoon_native | Both other candidates specified timing labels with no layout, so their holds existed only on paper. Tall-panel disclosure converts the label into actual reading time. |
| P01–P03 rhythm | webtoon_native | The opening burst makes the P04 hold land. Without it the scene has one speed. |

## Rejected

- **Webtoon-native as spine.** Its scroll design is the best in the set, but the panels specified
  shape without content — production could not draw from it. Kept the layout, replaced the interiors.
- **Cutting P06** (proposed by critic_emotional, minor). Kept, but re-shaped into a cramped sliver.
  The compression is what makes P07 read as a release; cutting it flattens the climax.
- **Full-width P05b** (webtoon_native). Spending the full-width device before the climax costs P07
  its only structural escalation. P05b is inset; P07 is the sole full-width panel.

## Overrides

- critic_webtoon_native scored `continuity: 7` on the grounds that no state was tracked. Overruled
  as an artifact of that candidate's scope rather than a defect of the direction — the synthesis
  carries the cinematic candidate's declared axis and light direction, which the Continuity
  Supervisor verified independently.

## Open risks

- P07 at 2.6 screen-heights is at the edge of what reads as one image. If production cannot hold
  the composition across two passes, split the darkness into its own panel rather than shortening
  the panel — the travel is the point.
- The name on the access card must be legible at mobile width in P05a. If the art cannot carry it,
  the dramatic irony collapses and the P05a/P05b split loses its reason to exist.
