# Stage 2 Handoff — sample_ep01 · "The Blue Pulse"

Production-neutral. No image model, art tool, or studio pipeline is assumed.

## Production priority
1. **P07** — the climax. Height, framing, and the single light source carry the scene.
2. **P05a** — the card must be readable at mobile width; the scene's dramatic irony depends on it.
3. **P05b** — the stopped hand. Do not show the face; the crop is the beat.

## Global visual direction
- **Tone:** restrained, procedural, cold — a workplace that turns wrong quietly.
- **Contrast:** flat and institutional in the corridor (P01–P02); high-contrast, single-source
  from P04 onward. The change of contrast is the change of world.
- **Camera language:** progressively tightening through the corridor, one release to wide at P07.
  Axis held south; the P06 crossing is intentional.
- **Scroll language:** short burst → held tall panel → gap → hard insert → long hold → cramped
  sliver → 2.6-screen payoff. Total ≈ 9.4 screen heights.
- **Palette note:** the blue pulse is the only saturated colour in the episode. Everything else
  reads as cold neutral. If the palette changes, the reveal loses its signal.

## Character consistency constraints
- Mina: field jacket over lab shirt, ID lanyard tucked in, hair tied back.
- Tablet stays in her **left** hand in every panel; she reaches with the **right** at P05b.
- No injuries or costume changes in this scene.

## Panel instructions
See `panel_direction.yaml` — every panel carries shot, angle, composition, blocking, gaze,
foreground/background, lighting, dialogue space, timing, whitespace, transition, and purpose.

## Continuity source of truth
See `continuity_state.yaml`. Two accepted minor items:
- Corridor fill in P05b assumes the south door stays open.
- The axis crossing at P06 is declared, not accidental.

## Critical warnings
- **Do not add dialogue.** The scene carries exactly one spoken word by design. Any line added at
  the art stage will explain an image that already works.
- **Do not shorten P07.** If the panel cannot hold at 2.6 screens, split the darkness into its own
  panel — the reader's travel through the dark is the beat, not the picture at the end of it.
- **Do not light Mina from the left inside the lab.** The only key is the north alcove from P04 on.
- **P07 is the only full-width panel.** Adding another anywhere in the episode costs the climax its
  structural escalation.
