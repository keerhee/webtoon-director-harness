# Direction Bible — sample_ep01 · "The Blue Pulse"

Approved at loop 2 · weighted score **8.85 / 10** (threshold 8.5) · continuity: 0 blocking, 0 major

## 1. Creative North Star
- **Scene objective:** Mina learns the prototype she certified as destroyed is running, and that
  someone she trusts was here first.
- **Reader emotion arc:** routine → unease → dread → recognition → shock
- **Core reveal:** Jun's access card (P05a/P05b), then the powered prototype (P07).
- **Climactic image:** P07 — Mina a silhouette at the bottom of a 2.6-screen panel, the prototype
  the only light source in the room.

## 2. Direction Strategy
- **Selected approach:** synthesis — cinematic spine, emotional turn, webtoon-native rhythm.
- **Why:** cinematic was the only candidate that established geography, which every close-up in the
  other two depends on. It could not, by itself, make the reader *feel* the turn.
- **Borrowed from alternates:** the P03 face and the P05a/P05b split (emotional); panel heights,
  whitespace, and the P07 tall-panel disclosure (webtoon-native).

## 5. Dialogue & Silence
- **Preserve:** "…Jun." (P05b) — the only spoken line in the scene.
- **Compress:** the original four-line recognition speech collapses into that one word.
- **Silent beats:** every other panel. Silence is the scene's default state, not an effect.
- **SFX:** a low hum at P04 only — sound arrives before its source is seen.

## 6. Continuity Constraints
- **Character:** Mina — field jacket, lanyard tucked, hair tied back. Tablet in her **left** hand
  throughout; she reaches with the **right** at P05b.
- **Props:** Jun's access card on the floor two metres inside the door from P05a. Do not move it.
- **Location:** door south, workbench centre, cable rack east, alcove north.
- **Lighting:** corridor light is cut at the threshold. From P04, the only key is the blue pulse
  from the **north** alcove — never light her from the left in the lab.
- **Camera axis:** held south through P05b; the crossing at P06 is deliberate and legal because
  P06 is a neutral over-shoulder.
- **Timeline:** ~70 seconds, starting 22:40.

## 7. Production Notes
- **Must preserve:** the P05a/P05b split; the card's name legible at mobile width; P07 as the only
  full-width panel; P07's height (the travel *is* the beat).
- **Flexible:** exact cable-rack dressing; the corridor's background detail; P06's width.
- **Risks:** P07 at 2.6 screens sits at the edge of reading as one image — if it cannot hold, split
  the darkness into its own panel rather than shortening it. If the card name cannot be made legible,
  the scene's only dramatic irony collapses and P05a/P05b must be reconsidered.
