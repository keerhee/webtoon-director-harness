# Panel Direction

For every panel define:
`panel_id`, `dramatic_function`, `shot`, `angle`, `composition`, `character_blocking`, `gaze`, `foreground`, `background`, `lighting`, `dialogue_space`, `beat_duration`, `whitespace_before`, `whitespace_after`, `transition`, `purpose`.

Do not choose camera language decoratively. Every decision must support story, emotion, rhythm, or readability.
